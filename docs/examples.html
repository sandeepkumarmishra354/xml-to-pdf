Examples

Complete documents from the examples/ folder, with their rendered output.

Hello world

The smallest useful document: a constant, a named style, a footer with page numbers, a paragraph and a table.

<?xml version="1.0" encoding="UTF-8"?>
<document title="Hello" page-size="A5" margin="15mm">
  <constants>
    <const name="name" value="World"/>
  </constants>

  <resources>
    <style id="title" size="24" bold="true" color="#1f4e9c" margin-bottom="8"/>
  </resources>

  <footer><text align="center" size="8" color="gray">Page {page} of {pages}</text></footer>

  <page>
    <text style="title">Hello, ${name}!</text>
    <text margin-bottom="10">This PDF was generated from XML. Text wraps automatically,
      and you can mix <b>bold</b>, <i>italic</i> and <span color="red">colored</span> runs.</text>
    <table widths="2*,1*" header-bg="#eef2f9">
      <row header="true"><cell>Feature</cell><cell>Status</cell></row>
      <row><cell>Text, images, tables</cell><cell>Ready</cell></row>
      <row><cell>Watermarks, headers, footers</cell><cell>Ready</cell></row>
    </table>
  </page>
</document>

Invoice

This example shows a data-driven document. Customer details and line items come from a JSON file, and the item rows are generated with <for-each>. It also uses:

  • an absolutely positioned title block
  • a borderless two-column table for the addresses
  • a striped item table with a colored header
  • a DRAFT watermark whose text comes from a variable
  • a header that only appears from page 2 onwards
xml2pdf examples/invoice.xml --data examples/invoice.json -o invoice.pdf
xml2pdf examples/invoice.xml --data examples/invoice.json --set status=PAID

invoice.xml

<?xml version="1.0" encoding="UTF-8"?>
<!--
  Render with:
    xml2pdf examples/invoice.xml -data examples/invoice.json -o invoice.pdf
  Override any value from the command line:
    xml2pdf examples/invoice.xml -data examples/invoice.json -set status=PAID
  (XML comments may not contain a double dash, hence the single-dash flags.)
-->
<document title="Invoice ${invoice_no}" author="${company}"
          page-size="A4" margin="20mm" font="helvetica" size="10" color="#222">

  <constants>
    <const name="company" value="Acme Corp"/>
    <const name="company_address" value="12 Industrial Way, Springfield"/>
    <const name="currency" value="$"/>
    <const name="status" value="DRAFT"/>
    <const name="brand" value="#1f4e9c"/>
  </constants>

  <resources>
    <image id="logo" src="logo.png"/>
    <style id="muted" color="#777" size="9"/>
    <style id="h1" size="22" bold="true" color="${brand}"/>
    <style id="h2" size="12" bold="true" color="${brand}" margin-top="14" margin-bottom="6"/>
    <style id="num" align="right"/>
  </resources>

  <watermark text="${status}" opacity="0.12" rotate="45" size="110" color="${brand}"/>

  <header pages="2-">
    <text style="muted" align="right">${company} · Invoice ${invoice_no}</text>
  </header>

  <footer>
    <text style="muted" align="center">Page {page} of {pages}</text>
  </footer>

  <page>
    <img ref="logo" width="45mm"/>
    <!-- Absolutely positioned block in the top right corner. -->
    <text x="120mm" y="20mm" width="70mm" align="right" style="h1">INVOICE</text>
    <text x="120mm" y="30mm" width="70mm" align="right">
      No. <b>${invoice_no}</b><br/>
      Date: ${date}<br/>
      Due: ${due_date}
    </text>

    <spacer height="10"/>
    <table widths="1*,1*" border="0" padding="0">
      <row>
        <cell>
          <text style="muted">FROM</text>
          <text bold="true">${company}</text>
          <text>${company_address}</text>
        </cell>
        <cell>
          <text style="muted">BILL TO</text>
          <text bold="true">${customer.name}</text>
          <text>${customer.address}</text>
        </cell>
      </row>
    </table>

    <text style="h2">Items</text>
    <table widths="4*,1*,1.5*,1.5*" border="0.5" border-color="#bbb" padding="5 6"
           header-bg="${brand}" stripe="#f2f5fa">
      <row header="true" color="white">
        <cell>Description</cell>
        <cell align="right">Qty</cell>
        <cell align="right">Unit price</cell>
        <cell align="right">Amount</cell>
      </row>
      <!-- One row per element of the "items" array in invoice.json. -->
      <for-each items="${items}" var="item">
        <row>
          <cell>${item.name}<br/><span style="muted">${item.note}</span></cell>
          <cell style="num">${item.qty}</cell>
          <cell style="num">${currency}${item.price}</cell>
          <cell style="num">${currency}${item.amount}</cell>
        </row>
      </for-each>
      <row bold="true">
        <cell colspan="3" align="right">Total</cell>
        <cell style="num">${currency}${total}</cell>
      </row>
    </table>

    <text style="h2">Notes</text>
    <text align="justify">
      Thank you for your business. Payment is due within <b>30 days</b> of the invoice date.
      Please include the invoice number <i>${invoice_no}</i> with your payment. Late payments
      may be subject to a fee of <u>1.5% per month</u>.
    </text>
  </page>
</document>

Multi-page report

A 5-page report that shows layout features:

  • a cover page without a header
  • a table that continues across pages, repeating its header row
  • a header from page 2 onwards
  • a back-layer image watermark and a front "CONFIDENTIAL" stamp on every page
  • an explicit page break
  • long-word breaking and Latin-1 text
  • a final landscape page with its own margin
xml2pdf examples/report.xml
